Meet Pooja

LilPetal Crafts was started by Pooja with a profound passion for creating beautiful handmade gifts. What began as a small hobby of making gifts for friends and family quickly blossomed into a local brand dedicated to making every occasion special.

"Every bouquet and hamper is crafted carefully to make your special moments unforgettable. I believe that a gift should not just be an item, but a feeling—a memory wrapped in love."
